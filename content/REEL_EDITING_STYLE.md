## Rule: news = newspaper graphic
Whenever the script mentions something **from the news** (RBI policy, SEBI rule,
budget, IPO, market crash or rally, AI company news, etc.), use the
**newspaper-style graphic** from the reference carousel (around slide 8) for that segment.

Until screenshots are in, use this default version of the look:
- Off-white or aged newsprint background with paper texture
- Bold serif headline (like a front page), plus a small masthead/date line
- Highlight 2-4 key words (yellow marker or red underline)
- Animate in quickly: a zoom/slide in, or a "paper drop" with a paper-rustle SFX
- Keep it on screen for 2-4 seconds, long enough to read the headline
- Name the real source and date on screen (e.g. "Source: RBI, Nov 2026").
  Never invent headlines or make them look like a real paper's front page.
